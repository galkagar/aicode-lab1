# Grad Run: Outrun the AI

A Temple Run–style browser game for AICODE Lab 1. You play a student running across campus, chased by an AI that wants your future job.

- **Collect** company logos (each one counts as a job application), 💯/A+ tests, ☕ coffee (magnet) and 🛡️ shields (deflect the next hit).
- **Fill the AI-skills bar** (Prompting, PyTorch, RAG, …) and the tables turn: the camera pulls back, the AI flees ahead, and you chase it with a whip for big points. The bar becomes the timer.
- **Avoid** DEADLINE hurdles (jump), PLAGIARISM SCAN lasers (slide), REJECTED boxes (change lanes) and Exam Week pits (jump).
- **Turn** left or right at the junctions. If you trip twice in a row, the AI catches you.

Controls: arrow keys or WASD (Space also jumps), or swipe on a phone. During the chase, ↑ / Space / tap cracks the whip. Press P to pause.

The game is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on a canvas, with no backend, so GitHub Pages can host it as is.
